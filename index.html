01 / About

An engineer's toolkit with a strategist's questions.

I'm studying Computer Science and Business Administration at the University of Southern California. Before USC, I served in the Republic of Singapore Navy as a communications sergeant and led technical projects for clients at a Singapore venture firm.

Most of my research happens at the National Institute of Education, Singapore. I deploy IoT sensors across school sites, run field studies, and help get papers to conferences on time. My work looks at how students' environment and physiology shape the way they learn.

I've presented this work in the United States, France, Taiwan and Singapore, and I like problems where the technical answer comes with a real case for why it matters.

    Development of an Automated Microclimate Adjustment System based on Concentration Levels of Students

    J. Koh, P. Thangaraja & K. Y. T. Lim

    14th International Conference on Applied Human Factors and Ergonomics (AHFE 2023), San Francisco · Cognitive Computing and Internet of Things

    A classroom system that uses computer vision and machine learning to gauge how well students are concentrating, then adjusts the room's microclimate to match. A NodeMCU microcontroller drives the air conditioner through infrared codes, making full use of its modes to keep students comfortable while saving energy.
